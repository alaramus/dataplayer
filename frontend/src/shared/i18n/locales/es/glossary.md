# Spanish: terms and style

One word for one thing, across every screen. Add a term here before using a
second word for it.

## Style

- Neutral Spanish (`es`), Quasar's `es` pack, ECharts' `ES` pack.
- Write natural Spanish. A label is short and noun-like: «Conexión», «Guardar»,
  «Cancelar», «Eliminar».
- A button or menu item is an action, usually an infinitive: «Guardar»,
  «Eliminar», «Abrir», «Cancelar».
- Address the reader impersonally where possible: «No se pudo guardar el
  archivo». Use «tú» only where a full sentence must address one person, and
  keep it consistent within a message.
- Punctuation: «,», «.», «?», «:», «;». Quotes are «…» or “…” and ‘…’. A dash
  is «—», an ellipsis is «…». A colon in a label may be «:».
- Keep one space between Latin text and the sentence: «conexión PostgreSQL»,
  «archivo CSV», «{n} filas».
- Names a person typed (a table, a connection) stay as they are, in quotes.
- Product and protocol names stay in Latin: PostgreSQL, TimescaleDB, Redis,
  SSH, S3, SQL, CSV, JSON, DuckDB, Docker.
- Do not translate code, identifiers, SQL, key names, enum values, or a
  `{placeholder}`. A placeholder is kept exactly: `{name}`, `{count}`, `{table}`.
  Never add, remove, rename or reorder its braces.
- A message's plural forms are `one | other`, in that order. Spanish «one» is
  1; everything else is «other». Keep both forms even when they read alike.
- Never write a bare `@` or `$` in a vue-i18n message.
- Use digits (`5`) and a comma as the decimal separator in prose: `1,5`.
