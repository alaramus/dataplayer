# German: terms and style

One word for one thing, across every screen. Add a term here before using a
second word for it.

## Style

- Sentence case: only the first word and nouns are capitalized: „Verbindung
  öffnen“, not „Verbindung Öffnen“.
- A button or menu item is an infinitive: „Speichern“, „Löschen“, „Öffnen“.
- The reader is addressed with formal „Sie“ only where a full sentence must
  address them; impersonal forms are preferred: „Die Datei konnte nicht
  gespeichert werden“ rather than „Sie konnten die Datei nicht speichern“.
- Quotes are „…“; a dash is „–“ or „—“; an ellipsis is „…“.
- Names a person typed (a table, a connection) stay as they are, in quotes.
- Product and protocol names stay in Latin: PostgreSQL, Redis, SSH, S3, SQL,
  CSV, JSON.
- A count uses the plural forms `one | other`: `{n} Zeile | {n} Zeilen`.
