# Russian: terms and style

One word for one thing, across every screen. Add a term here before using a
second word for it.

## Style

- Sentence case: «Новое подключение», not «Новое Подключение».
- A button or menu item is an infinitive: «Сохранить», «Удалить», «Открыть».
- The reader is addressed as «вы», lower case, and only when it cannot be
  avoided; impersonal forms are preferred: «Не удалось сохранить».
- Quotes are «ёлочки»; a dash is «—» with spaces; an ellipsis is «…».
- Names a person typed (a table, a connection) stay as they are, in quotes.
- Product and protocol names stay in Latin: PostgreSQL, Redis, SSH, S3, SQL,
  CSV, JSON.
- A count uses the plural forms `one | few | many | other`:
  `{n} строка | {n} строки | {n} строк | {n} строки`.
